01 · Brand Foundations
The Story of
Tintabudi

The Name

Tinta — from Spanish, meaning ink. A metaphor for the written word, for language, for thought made permanent.

Budi — from Sanskrit buddhi, meaning wisdom, understanding, intellect. The mind that receives and transforms.

The Mission

A curated neighbourhood bookshop that celebrates the lifelong pursuit of knowledge. Tintabudi is where the thirst for wisdom is quenched — a gathering place for curious minds, radical thinkers, and devoted readers in Taman Tun Dr Ismail, Kuala Lumpur.

The Positioning

"Good books light up our world."

Tintabudi occupies the space between scholar and neighbourhood friend — intellectually rigorous yet never intimidating. Independent, curated, and deeply rooted in Malaysiana and Southeast Asian intellectual life.

02 · Logo System
The Mark

The Tintabudi logomark is a synthesis of a pen nib and an organic, flowing form — representing the act of writing (tinta/ink) and the grace of wisdom (budi). The wordmark is set in uppercase using a condensed, structured typeface to ground the mark with confidence.

07 · Voice & Tone
How We Speak

Tintabudi speaks like a well-read friend — warm, considered, never condescending. We are confident in our curation without being prescriptive. We invite. We don't push.

✦ Write Like This

A curated selection of books that challenge how you see the world. Philosophy, history, literature — and everything in between.
We're closed on Mondays. Back Tuesday, 10:30am — with fresh arrivals and strong opinions.
Tintabudi celebrates the individual's lifelong pursuit of knowledge. Come in. Linger. Discover something you didn't know you needed.

Writing Principles

Be Literary

Draw from the language of books — evocative, precise, a little poetic. Avoid corporate and transactional language entirely.

Be Human

We are booksellers first, marketers second. Every piece of copy should feel like it was written by someone who genuinely loves books.

Be Measured

Fewer words, more meaning. Restrain the urge to over-explain. Trust the reader's intelligence. Leave space for discovery.
